How to use Meter Reading & Billing

A quick guide to adding readings, managing outstanding balances, editing records, exporting images, and reading the exported PNG correctly.

1. Adding a record

Fill in the four fields at the top of the main page:

FieldWhat to enter
Customer Name The customer's name (free text).
Previous Unit The meter reading from last month.
Current Unit This month's reading. Must be higher than the previous unit.
Previous Outstanding (₦) Optional. Any amount the customer already owed from before this month. Leave empty for ₦0.

Then click Add Record, or press Enter in any field.

Validation: the app will refuse to save if the current unit is not strictly greater than the previous unit, or if the outstanding figure is negative.

2. Understanding the columns

ColumnMeaning
Previous / CurrentThe two meter readings.
UsedUnits consumed = Current − Previous.
Cost (₦)Exact cost = Used × Rate per unit.
Payable (₦) Cost rounded to the nearest ₦5, so the customer pays a cash-friendly figure. The shift from the exact cost is at most ±₦2.
Owed (₦) Previous outstanding balance entered at the time the record was saved.
Balance (₦) Payable + Owed — the full amount the customer owes now.
Summary A one-paragraph plain-English description of the record.

Worked example

Rate
₦250 per unit
Previous
1,200 units
Current
1,345 units
Used
145 units
Cost
145 × ₦250 = ₦36,250
Payable
Rounded to nearest ₦5 = ₦36,250
Owed
₦5,000
Balance
₦36,250 + ₦5,000 = ₦41,250
Why round to ₦5? ₦1 and ₦2 coins are rarely in circulation, so rounding the payable figure to the nearest ₦5 keeps the amount cash-friendly. The shift is tiny — at most ₦2 either way — and the Cost column always shows the exact amount, so nothing is hidden.

3. Reading the totals

The exported image shows three summary figures under the title (each is the sum across all saved records):

FigureWhat it sums
Units used Every row's Used column added together.
Total owed Every row's Owed column added together (previous arrears only).
Total Every row's Balance column added together — i.e. Total Payable + Total Owed. This is the overall amount receivable across all customers.
Don't confuse "Total owed" with "Total": Total owed is only what was carried over from before; Total is everything the customers owe now (this month's charge + arrears).

4. Navigating the exported PNG

When you click Download PNG or Share Image, the app draws one image containing everything on screen. Here is how to read it, top to bottom:

① Title bar "Meter Reading & Billing Records" — just the heading of the sheet.
② Summary line Shows Generated date · record count · rate per unit · Units used · Total owed · Total. These three figures are your headline totals — see section 3 above for what each one means.
③ Table Every saved record, one row per customer, newest at the top. Columns run left to right: S/N, Customer Name, Previous, Current, Used, Cost, Payable, Owed, Balance, Summary. Money columns are highlighted in cyan.

Quick tips when scanning the PNG

  • Looking for one customer? Read down the Customer Name column. The order matches the app (newest first).
  • Looking for who owes the most? Sort mentally by the Balance (₦) column — that is the number a customer must clear to be fully paid up (this month + arrears).
  • Need only the arrears figure? Use the Owed (₦) column. Rows with — have no prior balance.
  • Checking why a total is what it is? Add up the Balance (₦) column of every row — it will equal the Total in the summary line. Add up Owed (₦) only and you'll get the Total owed. They are not the same figure.
  • Zooming on mobile: pinch to zoom into the PNG like any photo. Text stays sharp because the image is rendered at 2× resolution.
The PNG is a snapshot. If you add, edit, or delete records afterwards, you must download a new PNG to reflect the changes — the file already saved on your device will not update.

5. Editing a customer name

  1. Click the customer name in the table (underlined with a dotted line).
  2. A small editor appears under the name with the current name filled in.
  3. Type the new name and click Update.
  4. Click Cancel to leave it unchanged.
Only the name can be edited this way — not the units, cost, or outstanding balance. Those are locked once saved so a past bill cannot be silently altered. If a reading was entered wrong, delete the row and re-add it.

6. Deleting a record

  1. Find the × button at the end of the row.
  2. Click it. A confirmation dialog appears.
  3. Click OK to permanently remove the record, or Cancel to keep it.

7. Downloading and sharing

Download PNG

Saves the whole table as a single image named meter-records-YYYY-MM-DD.png. Perfect for printing or archiving. See section 4 for how to read the image.

Share Image

Opens your device's share sheet (WhatsApp, Telegram, email, etc.). If your browser does not support sharing files, the image will be downloaded instead.

8. Changing the rate per unit

Click the rate number shown in the subtitle (e.g. "250 Naira") to open the rate editor. Enter a new value and click Update.

Changing the rate affects only new records. Existing records keep the cost they were saved with, so past bills are never recalculated.

9. Clearing all records

Click Clear Previous Records. You will be asked to confirm. Once confirmed, every record is permanently deleted from this device and cannot be recovered.

Back up first. Download a PNG before clearing, in case you need to refer to the records later.

10. Where data is stored

All records are saved in your browser's Storage, on this device only. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Clearing your browser data, switching browsers, or switching devices will not carry the records over.
